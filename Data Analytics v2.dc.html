User Report Summary Behavior
Player cast Yuumi's passive shield and was interrupted mid-cast. The shield applied on the client but never registered server-side, so the ally took full damage through a shield that was visibly on them.
0:15
See more details
Analyzed Problem
Cursor Agent
Based on the replay clip and player input history, this looks like a client/server state desync on cast interrupt — not a rendering issue.
When the cast is interrupted, YuumiPassive::onCastComplete() applies the shield locally before AbilityState::syncToServer() has confirmed it, so an interrupt leaves the two states diverged. Likely source: src/Game/Champions/Yuumi/YuumiPassive.cpp around lines 418–461, called from ChampionController.cpp. Watch Vandiril’s recording through the cast interrupt to confirm.
