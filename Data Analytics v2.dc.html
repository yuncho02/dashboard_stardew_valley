Based on the replay clip and player input history, this looks like a collision / interaction ordering bug — not a rendering issue.
When the axe is active, FarmhouseDoor::tryEnter() returns early without clearing the pending move, while TreeCollider::resolveOverlap() still pushes the character into the tree’s hitbox.
