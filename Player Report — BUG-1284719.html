Yuumi passive desync on cast interrupt
@BerniceSun02 player · Aug 7, 2026 · Champion behaviour · Bernice
What the player reported Behavior
Player cast Yuumi’s passive shield and was interrupted mid-cast. The shield applied on the client but never registered server-side, so the ally took full damage through a shield that was visibly on them.
Ghost AI analysis Cursor Agent
Based on the replay clip and player input history, this looks like a client/server state desync on cast interrupt — not a rendering issue. When the cast is interrupted, YuumiPassive::onCastComplete() applies the shield locally before AbilityState::syncToServer() has confirmed it, so an interrupt leaves the two states diverged. Reproduce by attaching to an ally, starting the passive cast, then taking a knockup mid-cast. src/Game/Champions/Yuumi/YuumiPassive.cpp:418–461
